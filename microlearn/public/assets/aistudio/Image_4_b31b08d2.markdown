## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, grounded biology laboratory aesthetic designed for active classroom inquiry, specimen exploration, and peer observation. The visual tone bridges tactile field notes, benchtop optical instruments, and contemporary educational clarity. It avoids cold, detached clinical sterilities, synthetic neon gradients, and tech-heavy AI archetypes.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for secondary and undergraduate biology students, lab instructors, and workshop facilitators. The interface creates an atmosphere of grounded curiosity, calm focus, and physical utility. Controls feel tangible, robust, and readable from an arm’s length at a laboratory workstation or a shared bench display.

### Aesthetic Foundation: Warm Tactile Naturalist
- **Surface Atmosphere:** Soft bone-parchment and chalked limestone tones provide a warm, low-glare canvas that reduces eye strain under fluorescent lab lighting.
- **Instrument Precision:** Crisp slate and charcoal contours evoke field notebook grids, glass slides, and powder-coated microscope bodies.
- **Biological Vitality:** Organic sage, moss, and warm amber serve functional annotation roles—directing gaze without visual fatigue.
- **Tactile Affordance:** Gentle inward-recessed viewports, mechanical toggles, distinct bezel frames, and hair-thin reticle overlays evoke analog optical apparatuses translated into a modern digital tool.

## Colors

The palette derives from physical specimen collection: pressed foliage, raw field binders, optical brass, and dark slate workbenches.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2D5A43` — Deep Botanical Sage):** The grounding organic accent. Used for primary calls-to-action, active specimen tabs, confirmed biological classifications, and primary status rings.
- **Secondary (`#D97724` — Lab Amber):** High-contrast warning, specimen flag, dynamic focus alerts, and live recording badges. It carries warm visibility without panic or clinical alarm.
- **Tertiary (`#4A6B6C` — Slate Celadon):** Analytical secondary metrics, scale bars, reticle coordinate markers, and measurement overlays.
- **Neutral (`#202623` — Charcoal Shingle):** High-density text, slide borders, and microscope framing elements. Carries a slight woodland undertone to eliminate harsh digital black.

### Surface and Canvas Distribution
- **App Background (`#F5F3ED` — Warm Limestone):** The global backdrop. Keeps the experience warm, matte, and paper-like.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#FFFFFF` — Crisp Parchment):** Specimen data cards, observation decks, slide metadata panels.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#EAE6DC` — Prepared Slide Grey):** Inset controls, nested specimen counters, inactive viewport wells.
- **Optical Inset / Viewports (`#111613` — Stage Dark):** Ultra-dark biological stage tone dedicated exclusively to live camera feeds, fluorescent imaging, and digital microscope viewports.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes immediate recognition at varied working distances:
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides geometric warmth with friendly, sculpted apertures. Balances modern software efficiency with human approachability.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse engineered for dense taxonomic data, student lab instructions, and observation logs.
- **Analytical Overlays (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced numeric feedback for magnification levels (e.g., `400x`), micron scales (`50µm`), shutter speeds, stage coordinates, and sample timestamps. Keeps instrument values aligned and legible against active visual feeds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system tuned for asymmetric workstation screens, bench tablets, and dynamic instrument split-views.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. The primary viewport stage claims 8 columns, while data readouts and note panels claim 4 columns.
- **Tablet Landscape & Split Benches (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Camera viewport docks to the upper half or 5 columns; taxonomic panels stack alongside with `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Microscope controls collapse into horizontal bottom slide-sheets.

### Spacing Cadence
Component internals use consistent mathematical steps:
- `space-xs` (4px): Metric label badges, crosshair tick marks, status dots.
- `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-text distances, chip horizontal padding, segmented button gaps.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard form element padding, card interior margins, list row spacing.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card headers, toolbars, split partition separators.
- `space-xl` (36px) & `space-2xl` (56px): Primary section divisions and lab module boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic floating layers and multi-tiered diffuse shadows. Depth is communicated through structural card borders, tactile debossed wells, and ambient, warm-tinted grounding.

### Layer Hierarchy
1. **Base Field (Level 0):** `#F5F3ED` matte surface canvas.
2. **Structural Panels (Level 1):** Solid white `#FFFFFF` cards bounded by a `1px` subtle rim border (`rgba(32, 38, 35, 0.08)`). Grounded with a soft contact shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(32, 38, 35, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(32, 38, 35, 0.02)`.
3. **Interactive & Lifted Objects (Level 2):** Hovered specimen trays and active dialogs use an ambient botanical shadow: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(32, 38, 35, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(32, 38, 35, 0.04)`.
4. **Debossed Instrument Wells (Recessed):** Tool palettes, measurement docks, and feed viewports use inset borders and directional inner shading: `inset 0 1px 3px rgba(32, 38, 35, 0.12)`.
5. **Feed Viewport Frames:** Beveled outer casing (`#202623`) with a crisp inner edge highlight (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) enclosing the black sensor stage.

## Shapes

The shape system employs an intentional balance between organic softness and optical instrument precision.

### Curvature Rules
- **Standard Corners (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form fields, nested metric cells, tags, and secondary action buttons.
- **Card Frames & Viewports (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** Outer boundaries for camera feeds, inspection trays, and taxonomy dossiers. Soft enough to feel friendly; tight enough to feel technical.
- **Overlays & Dialogs (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Floating observation modals and student assignment panels.
- **Pill Geometry (Full Radius):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, active live tags, magnification pills (`10x`, `40x`, `100x`), and floating slide stage counters.

## Components

### 1. Optical Camera Viewports & Reticles
- **Surface:** Dark background `#111613` housed within a 16px rounded frame.
- **Reticle Markings:** Hairline overlays (`1px` width, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`) marking center alignment crosshairs, concentric target circles, and rule-of-thirds composition grids.
- **Scale Legend:** Bottom-left pinned `metric-reticle` display with physical calibration bars (e.g., `|--- 20 µm ---|`) in white or slate celadon.
- **HUD Badges:** Pill badges inside the viewport using translucent deep slate backgrounds (`rgba(17, 22, 19, 0.72)`) with a `1px` border for magnification, lighting mode, and sensor FPS.

### 2. Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled deep botanical sage (`#2D5A43`) with white text. Height: 44px (touch target minimum for lab tablets). 8px roundedness. Hover state dims to `#234735` with no artificial glow.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface tint `#EAE6DC` with `#202623` text and a subtle 1px border (`rgba(32, 38, 35, 0.12)`).
- **Amber Action Button:** Reserved for capture, sample pinning, or urgent recalibration (`#D97724`).

### 3. Metric Chips & Magnification Selectors
- **Magnification Stepper:** Segmented group styled as a tactile recessed toggle track. Active segment has a white pill card with a faint `0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` shadow and deep sage text. Inactive segments use muted charcoal text.
- **Classification Chips:** Soft parchment backgrounds with left-aligned 6px status dots (e.g., Green for Plantae, Amber for Pending Verification).

### 4. Input Fields & Lab Log Notes
- **Text Inputs:** Height: 44px. Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1.5px` solid `#DCD7CA`. Focus state transitions border to `#2D5A43` with an outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(45, 90, 67, 0.15)`.
- **Placeholder:** `#727B75` tone.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Form:** 20px square with 4px corner radius for checkboxes; 20px circle for radio controls.
- **Selected State:** Solid `#2D5A43` with a clean white vector check or inner white bullseye (8px).
- **Unselected:** Clean white background with a 1.5px outline in `#B5B0A2`.

### 6. Cards & Specimen Trays
- **Specimen Dossier Card:** White base, 16px corner radius, 16px internal padding. Header carries a subtle bottom boundary line (`1px solid #ECE8DE`).
- **Interactive List Rows:** Alternating hover highlight using `#F5F3ED` with a 4px left-edge indicator strip in primary sage upon selection.

### 7. Specialized Domain Components
- **Slide Stage Scrubber:** Horizontal scrubbing bar calibrated in micrometers with tick marks rendered using the label font.
- **Organelle / Feature Callout Pin:** Circular 24px semi-transparent amber or green marker anchored to sample coordinates, connected via a thin 1px hairline leader line to a floating text annotation badge.
